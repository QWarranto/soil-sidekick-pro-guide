# Compliance and Legal Documentation

**Last updated:** 2026-10-31
**Documentation revision:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Scope and assurance

This document describes compliance considerations for SoilSidekick Pro, SoilCertify and LeafEngines. It is not a legal opinion, certification report or guarantee that a particular integration meets a regulation. Requirements vary by data, jurisdiction, customer and use case.

## 2. Framework status

| Framework | Current documentation position |

Provider certifications and available compliance-support services are not certifications of the customer or this product.

## 3. Data handling and rights

Public datasets, field/sensor records, usage and personal information have different sensitivity and access requirements. Account/field access and encryption must be assessed against actual deployed controls. Local AI and hosted AI are different processing paths; hosted requests can transmit selected context to a provider.

Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection requests must be handled through actual available account functions or the approved privacy/support channel. Do not rely on an unverified deletion deadline, backup period, audit-retention duration, DPA URL or named representative from an older document. Applicable policies and contracts control.

## 4. Operational and agronomic boundaries

Soil and satellite information can be estimates or published regional context. Water observations can be cached/historical/incomplete. Recommendations are not laboratory certification, guaranteed yield, underwriting/claim adjudication or proof of a damage event.

Prescription drafts and format exports do not establish safe machine operation, controller approval or universal standards conformance. Qualified review and use-specific verification are required. Positional estimates and uncertainty indicators do not establish cryptographic proof of physical location or fraud prevention.

## 5. Intellectual property and licensing

Certain platform technologies are proprietary; integration packages have their own license terms. Patent applications, granted claims, copyright and trade-secret protection are distinct. This revision does not confirm a patent grant, its scope or any application's legal status. Request approved IP information before making reliance or licensing decisions.

Review the LICENSE file for the exact package/version. Do not assume all SDKs/adapters share one license, that generated client code conveys a license to the proprietary service, or that an SBOM has been published for every artifact.

## 6. Contracts and third parties

Service terms, order forms, applicable privacy notices and agreed SLAs govern service commitments. Confirm subprocessors, data regions, notice periods, retention and incident contacts from operative documents; old architecture lists are not authoritative current contracts.

Breach-notification duties depend on jurisdiction and assessed circumstances. GDPR authority notification can require action within 72 hours of awareness; customer notification is separately risk-dependent. Export-control classification, sanctions controls and enterprise exemptions require qualified review; no specific classification or universal geo-block is certified here.

## 7. Public statements and changes

Public claims require evidence and appropriate legal approval. Documentation dates do not establish earlier product behavior or amend a patent record. Future design proposals do not imply delivered capability. Internal methodology and patent details are not disclosed in this customer-facing reference.

The October 31 revision removes unsupported blanket certification, retention and IP-protection statements from this document. Legal/independent assurance review remains necessary before making those claims.
