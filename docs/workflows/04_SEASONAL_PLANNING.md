# Workflow 4: Seasonal Planning & Task Management

> **Goal:** Build a data-driven seasonal plan using task templates, the planting calendar, and AI recommendations.
> **Time:** ~20 minutes | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Tier:** Free (basic) / Starter+ (full)

---

```

Planning Assistant and Task Manager to build a complete plan for your

```

## Step 1: Open the Seasonal Planning Assistant

1. Navigate to **Seasonal Planning** from the main menu.
2. Select your county/location (if not already set).
3. The assistant generates region-specific seasonal recommendations.

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Go to Seasonal Planning and confirm your location. The assistant uses
your county's climate zone, frost dates, and growing season length to
build a personalized timeline."
```

---

## Step 2: Review the Planting Calendar

1. Navigate to **Planting Calendar**.
2. The calendar shows optimal windows for:
   - Cool-season crops (lettuce, peas, spinach)
   - Warm-season crops (corn, tomatoes, squash)
   - Cover crops and green manures
3. Each crop window includes:
   - Earliest safe planting date (based on last frost)
   - Optimal planting range
   - Latest recommended planting date
   - Days to maturity

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"The Planting Calendar calculates your optimal planting windows using
Multi-Parameter Planting Optimization. That means it's not just looking
at frost dates — it's factoring in soil temperature, moisture, and
your specific soil type."

[ON-SCREEN] Show the calendar view with crop windows highlighted.
```

---

## Step 3: Browse the Task Template Library

1. Go to **Task Manager** → **Task Library** tab.
2. Browse 20+ pre-built seasonal task templates:

### Spring Templates
- Soil Testing & Preparation
- Spring Planting (Cool & Warm Season)
- Irrigation System Setup
- Frost Protection Planning
- First Fertilizer Application

### Summer Templates
- Pest Scouting & IPM
- Irrigation Monitoring & Adjustment
- Mid-Season Fertilization
- Succession Planting
- Harvest Operations

### Fall/Winter Templates
- Fall Soil Amendment Application
- Cover Crop Planting
- Equipment Winterization
- Record Keeping & Season Review
- Winter Planning & Ordering

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"The Task Library gives you professionally designed templates for every
season. You don't have to start from scratch — pick the templates that
match your operation, customize the dates, and you're ready to go."

[ON-SCREEN] Scroll through the template library. Click into one to show details.
```

---

## Step 4: Add Tasks to Your Plan

1. Click **Add to My Tasks** on any template.
2. Customize the task:
   - **Date:** Adjust to your specific planting schedule.
   - **Field:** Assign to a specific field (if you've created them in Workflow 2).
   - **Notes:** Add your own reminders and details.
3. Repeat for all relevant seasonal tasks.

> 💡 **Tip:** Free tier allows up to 10 tasks per season. Starter tier unlocks unlimited tasks plus recurring task automation.

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Click 'Add to My Tasks' on any template. Set the date based on your
planting calendar, assign it to a field, and add any personal notes.
I like to add reminders about what went wrong last year — that way I
don't repeat mistakes."
```

---

## Step 5: Set Up Recurring Tasks (Starter+)

1. When creating or editing a task, toggle **Recurring** on.
2. Choose a frequency:
   - **Annual:** Task repeats yearly (e.g., "Spring Soil Testing").
   - **Seasonal (Pro+):** Repeats quarterly.
   - **Monthly (Pro+):** For ongoing monitoring tasks.
   - **Custom (Pro+):** Set your own interval.
3. The system automatically generates new task instances at the right time.

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Recurring tasks are a game changer. Set up 'Spring Soil Testing' as
an annual task once, and the system reminds you every year. No more
forgetting until it's too late."
```

---

## Step 6: Track Progress Through the Season

1. Return to **Task Manager** → **My Tasks** tab.
2. Tasks are organized by status: **Pending**, **In Progress**, **Completed**.
3. Update status as you complete work:
   - Click a task → Mark as **In Progress** when you start.
   - Click **Complete** when finished.
   - Add completion notes and observations.
4. Review your task timeline to stay ahead of schedule.

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"During the season, check your Task Manager weekly. Move tasks to 'In
Progress' as you start them, and mark them complete when done. The
completion notes you add now become your knowledge base for next year."

[TRANSITION] "You now have a complete seasonal plan tied to real data.
Next, let's look at Variable Rate Technology to optimize your inputs."
```
