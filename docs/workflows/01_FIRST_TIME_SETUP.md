## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Navigate to [soilsidekickpro.com](https://soilsidekickpro.com) and click **Get Started**.
2. Sign up with email or Google authentication.
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard** — your central hub for all features.

## Step 2: Select Your County

1. From the Dashboard, click **Soil Analysis** in the navigation.
2. Use the **County Search** bar:
   - Type your county name (e.g., "Travis") or
   - Enter a FIPS code directly (e.g., `48453` for Travis County, TX).
3. Select the correct state from the dropdown if multiple matches appear.
4. Click your county to confirm the selection.

> 💡 **Tip:** FIPS codes give the most accurate results. Find yours at [census.gov](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files.html).

## Step 3: Review Your Soil Analysis

Once your county is selected, the analysis loads automatically. Review each section:

### 3a. pH Level
| Range | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| < 6.0 | Acidic ⚠️ | Apply lime 2–3 months before planting |
| 6.0–7.0 | Optimal ✅ | Maintain with balanced fertilization |
| > 7.0 | Alkaline ⚠️ | Add sulfur or organic matter |

### 3b. Organic Matter
- **Target:** 2–5% depending on crop type.
- Green indicator = optimal; yellow/red = needs attention.
- Click the metric for a detailed breakdown.

### 3c. NPK Nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium)
- Bar charts compare your levels against optimal ranges.
- Deficiency recommendations appear below each nutrient.

## Step 4: Understand the Recommendations

1. Scroll to the **Recommendations** section below the analysis.
2. Each recommendation includes:
   - **What to do** (e.g., "Apply 2 tons/acre agricultural lime")
   - **Why** (e.g., "Raise pH from 5.4 to target 6.2")
   - **When** (e.g., "2–3 months before spring planting")
3. Recommendations are crop-aware if you've selected a crop type.

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Scroll down to see your personalized recommendations. These aren't
generic — they're calculated from your county's actual USDA soil survey
data. Each one tells you what, why, and when."
```

## Step 5: Export Your First Report

1. Click the **Export PDF** button in the top-right of the analysis panel.
2. The report includes:
   - All soil metrics and charts
   - Personalized recommendations
   - Environmental risk summary
   - County reference data
3. Save the PDF or share it directly with your agronomist.

> 💡 **Tip:** Pro subscribers get enhanced exports with satellite data, environmental impact scores, and lender-ready formatting.
