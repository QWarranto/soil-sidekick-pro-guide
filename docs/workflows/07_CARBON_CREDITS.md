> **Goal:** Calculate your carbon credit potential, track sustainability scores, and understand the path to verified carbon credit monetization.
> **Time:** ~15 minutes | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Tier:** Pro+

## Step 1: Open the Carbon Credit Calculator

1. Navigate to **Carbon Credit Calculator** from the Features menu.
2. The calculator needs basic information about your operation.

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Open the Carbon Credit Calculator. This tool estimates how many carbon
credits your farm could generate based on your actual practices and soil
conditions."
```

---

## Step 2: Enter Field Information

1. **Field name:** Identify the field you're calculating for.
2. **Field size:** Enter acreage.
3. **Soil organic matter:** Enter your current organic matter percentage (from Workflow 1 soil analysis).
4. **Practices:** Select which carbon-sequestering practices you use:
   - Cover cropping
   - No-till or reduced tillage
   - Crop rotation with legumes
   - Compost application
   - Managed grazing
   - Agroforestry

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Enter your field details. The organic matter percentage from your soil
analysis is the key input here — that's a direct measurement of carbon
stored in your soil. Then check the practices you're actually using.
Be honest — verification will eventually require documentation."
```

---

## Step 3: Review Credit Calculation

The calculator outputs:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Credits earned** | Estimated CO₂-equivalent tons sequestered per year |
| **Dollar value** | Market value at current voluntary carbon credit prices |
| **Verification status** | Pending / Verified / Traded |
| **Confidence level** | How reliable the estimate is given available data |

### Typical ranges:
- **Cover cropping alone:** 0.3–0.5 credits/acre/year
- **No-till + cover crops:** 0.5–1.0 credits/acre/year
- **Full regenerative package:** 1.0–2.0 credits/acre/year
- **Current voluntary market price:** $15–$50 per credit

```
[VIDEO NARRATION]
"Here's your calculation. A 100-acre field with cover crops and no-till
might generate 50–100 carbon credits per year. At $25 per credit, that's
$1,250 to $2,500 in additional revenue — just for the practices you're
already using."

[ON-SCREEN] Highlight the credits earned and dollar value.
```

## Step 5: Understand the Verification Path

Carbon credits go through three stages:

| Stage | Status | What's Needed |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| **1. Estimated** | Pending | Calculator output; no verification yet |
| **2. Verified** | Verified | Third-party verification of practices and soil carbon |
| **3. Traded** | Completed | Listed and sold on voluntary carbon market |

### To move from Estimated → Verified:
- Maintain detailed records of conservation practices
- Take soil samples (baseline and annual)
- Work with a verification body (Verra, Gold Standard, etc.)
- SoilSidekick Pro's tracking data serves as supporting evidence
